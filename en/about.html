About us

We went through relocating to France ourselves and know exactly what our clients face.

Our story

We came to France, studied at French universities and went through everything our clients face: prefectures, banks, finding housing, insurance and starting a business. We know how exhausting it is when you don't know the system or the language.

VD Azur Conseil supports families, entrepreneurs and construction companies on the French Riviera, in Monaco, Paris and Toulouse. We handle the paperwork and the dealings with authorities so you can focus on your life and business.
