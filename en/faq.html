Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the task. After a short call we give you the exact price and timeline — before any work starts.

Is the first consultation paid?

No, the first 15 minutes are free: we look at your situation and tell you how we can help.

Are you lawyers (avocats)?

No. We are consultants for administrative and legal matters. When you need a lawyer's advice, court representation or a notarial act, we bring in our partner lawyers and notaries and coordinate their work.

Why do you offer so many different services?

We work within a network of trusted partners. We handle the core work ourselves and bring in the right specialist for the rest. For you, that means one contact instead of ten.

How long does paperwork take?

It depends on the document and how busy the offices are. We tell you what to expect up front and update you on every status change.

Can we work remotely?

Yes. Many matters are handled remotely; when needed, we attend appointments with you or on your behalf.

Where do you work?

Across the French Riviera, plus Paris and Toulouse. Other cities in France if travel costs are covered. We also support construction companies in Monaco.

Who are your clients?

Russian-speaking and international individuals, as well as French and international companies, especially in construction.

I'm a professional. Can I become a partner?

We'd be glad to. Message us and tell us about yourself — we'll discuss mutual referrals.

Tell us about your situation

We reply during working hours and tell you where to start. The first 15-minute consultation is free.
